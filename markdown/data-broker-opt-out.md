## About this letter

Use this letter to ask data brokers and people-search sites to remove
your personal information and stop selling it. Some states give you a
legal right to opt out; coverage varies by state and company.

## Letter

[DATE]

[YOUR FULL NAME]
[YOUR STREET ADDRESS]
[CITY, STATE, ZIP]
[YOUR EMAIL]
[YOUR PHONE]
[DATE OF BIRTH (OPTIONAL)]

[COMPANY NAME]
Attn: [ATTENTION]
[COMPANY STREET ADDRESS]
[COMPANY CITY, STATE, ZIP]

Re: Data removal and opt-out request — [YOUR FULL NAME] — [SITE OR PRODUCT NAME]

Dear [ATTENTION / COMPANY NAME],

I am writing to request that you remove my personal information from your databases, data products, and marketing lists, and stop selling or sharing it with third parties.

Please:

- Remove my listings and records from [SITE OR PRODUCT NAME] and any related sites or services you operate
- Stop collecting, selling, licensing, or sharing my personal information going forward
- Opt me out of future data collection by your company and its partners
- Confirm in writing when removal is complete

This request covers my name, current and past addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, date of birth, relatives, associates, and any other personal data you hold about me. [ADDITIONAL DETAILS]

If applicable, I am exercising my rights under privacy laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), the Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act (VCDPA), the Colorado Privacy Act (CPA), or the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Which laws apply depends on your location and situation.

If you need more information to process this request, contact me at [YOUR EMAIL] or [YOUR PHONE].

Sincerely,

[SIGNATURE]
[YOUR FULL NAME]
