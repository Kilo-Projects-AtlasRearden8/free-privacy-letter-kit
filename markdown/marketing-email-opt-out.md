## About this letter

Use this letter to tell companies to stop marketing emails to your
address. Under the federal CAN-SPAM Act, commercial email senders must
honor opt-out requests promptly; state laws may add more protections.

## Letter

[DATE]

[YOUR FULL NAME]
[YOUR STREET ADDRESS]
[CITY, STATE, ZIP]
[YOUR EMAIL]
[YOUR PHONE]

[COMPANY NAME]
Attn: [ATTENTION]
[COMPANY STREET ADDRESS]
[COMPANY CITY, STATE, ZIP]

Re: Marketing email opt-out — [YOUR EMAIL] — [MAILING LIST NAME]

Dear [ATTENTION / COMPANY NAME],

I am writing to request that you stop sending me marketing and promotional emails, and add my email address to your permanent suppression (do-not-mail) list.

Please:

- Stop all marketing and promotional emails to [YOUR EMAIL] effective immediately
- Remove my email address from all mailing lists under [COMPANY NAME], including shared or rented lists
- Apply this request to all brands, newsletters, and services you operate
- Confirm in writing once my opt-out has been processed

This request covers marketing email only; it does not apply to transactional emails (for example, receipts, password resets, or account notices) related to services I use.

Under the CAN-SPAM Act, commercial email senders must honor opt-out requests promptly and may not sell or transfer your address after you opt out, except as needed to comply with the law. Which rules apply depends on your location and situation.

If you need more information, contact me at [YOUR EMAIL] or [YOUR PHONE].

Sincerely,

[SIGNATURE]
[YOUR FULL NAME]
