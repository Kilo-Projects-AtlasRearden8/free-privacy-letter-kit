## About this letter

Use this letter to ask AI companies and platforms not to use your
content or personal data to train generative AI models. Companies are
not required by federal law to honor AI-training opt-outs, but many
offer them; check the company's privacy settings too.

Dear [ATTENTION / COMPANY NAME],

I am writing to opt out of AI training. Please do not use my content or personal data — including posts, uploads, messages, files, chat history, and any other material I have provided — to train, develop, fine-tune, or improve generative artificial intelligence models, now or in the future.

Please also:

- Exclude my content from AI training datasets and any third-party AI data sharing
- Stop using my personal data for AI model training, fine-tuning, or reinforcement learning
- Apply this opt-out to all products and services operated by [COMPANY NAME]
- Confirm in writing that my opt-out has been applied

If you need more information to process this request, contact me at [YOUR EMAIL] or [YOUR PHONE].

Where applicable, I am exercising my rights under privacy laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) or the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Which laws apply depends on your location and situation.
