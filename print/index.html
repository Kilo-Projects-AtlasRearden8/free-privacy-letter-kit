Free Privacy Letter Kit

Fillable, printable letters to opt out of AI training, data-broker lists, and marketing emails.
Designed by Asdale Dynamics, LLC — printable, no account needed. Education only, not legal advice.

What's inside

Three fillable letters you can type into on screen, then print (Ctrl+P / Cmd+P) or save as PDF. Nothing to sign up for, nothing to buy.

AI training opt-out letter

Ask AI companies not to use your content or personal data to train generative AI models.

Open letter

Data-broker opt-out letter

Ask data brokers and people-search sites to remove your personal information and stop selling it.

Open letter

Marketing email opt-out letter

Plain-text Markdown versions live in ../markdown/.

How to use

  1. Open a letter in your browser.
  2. Fill in the fields: your name, address, the company, and your account details.
  3. Print or save as PDF, then sign and send per the company's instructions.
  4. Keep a copy for your records.
